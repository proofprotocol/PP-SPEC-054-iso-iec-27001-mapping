# Contributing

PP-SPEC-054 is maintained as part of the Proof Protocol specification series.

Contributions may be submitted as issues or pull requests.

Contributions should preserve Proof Protocol's framework-agnostic architecture, distinguish ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 27002 concepts from Proof Protocol concepts, identify sources clearly, respect upstream licensing and trademarks, avoid reproducing protected normative text without permission, and preserve the normative meaning of the specification.

By contributing, you represent that you have the right to submit the contribution.
